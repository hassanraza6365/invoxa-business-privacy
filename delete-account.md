# Delete Your Invoxa Business Account

## Account Deletion Request

If you want to delete your Invoxa Business account and associated data, you can request deletion by contacting us.

### How to Request Account Deletion

Please send an email from the email address associated with your Invoxa Business account.

**Email:** YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL@example.com

Use the following subject:

**Delete My Invoxa Business Account**

Please include:

- Your Invoxa Business account email address
- Your request to permanently delete your account

We will process your account deletion request and delete the personal information associated with your account, subject to any information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reasons.

### What Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, we will delete or remove account-associated data that we are required to delete, including applicable:

- Account information
- Profile information
- Business information
- Customer information
- Invoice information
- Expense information
- Payment records
- Other personal information associated with your account

Some information may be retained for a limited period where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
